Proposed store copy, subject to final feature verification and app-name clearance:

**Name:** Still
**Subtitle:** A little room to read
**Category:** Books (proposal)
**Description:** Bring your PDF, EPUB or text and read at your own pace. Still displays words at a fixed point with adjustable average timing, or lets you switch to normal text. Revisit a sentence, review the surrounding paragraph, save passages and notes, and look up English words. Academic-paper citation controls are optional and preserve your original text. Your library is stored on your device. Export a bookshelf backup to move or keep your readings.

Limitations to retain: DRM-free EPUBs only; selectable-text PDFs; extraction/citation detection can need review; no Kindle/library service integration or OCR; no cloud sync; exported backups are readable JSON.
