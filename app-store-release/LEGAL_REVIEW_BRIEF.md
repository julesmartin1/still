# Legal and commercial review brief — unresolved, not legal clearance

The owner has explicitly left publisher identity, support email, countries and pricing undecided. No agreements have been accepted, public privacy claims submitted, purchases implemented or tax/banking information entered.

Prepared material: in-app privacy/help drafts, actual data-flow inventory, third-party notices, local import/export/deletion behavior, app-store copy and incident/support plan. Native runtime and archive verification must precede final legal/privacy representations.

Decisions needed from publisher/counsel:
1. Publisher legal identity and authority to distribute the code, name, icon and bundled sample. Conduct relevant name/trademark clearance in chosen markets. No clearance is claimed for “Still.”
2. Launch countries, intended age audience and whether the app is marketed to children or educational institutions. These facts determine which privacy, consumer, accessibility, trader/contact and children's rules need review. No blanket worldwide compliance declaration.
3. Free/paid/subscription model, entity/account arrangements and any tax/banking obligations. If paid features are chosen, review current regional Apple rules and add/test the appropriate StoreKit flow before release.
4. Public support/privacy website and mailbox; who receives support content; hosting providers/logging/retention; incident and rights-request handling. Existing private Sites access is not a usable public privacy-policy URL.
5. Final policy language for local processing, Apple device backups, plaintext export recipients, deletion limitations and optional Apple diagnostics. No invented “zero collection” statement without archive/traffic confirmation.
6. Whether Apple's standard EULA suffices or custom terms are needed. Draft product terms should describe permitted local import, rights retained by content owners, actual limitations, publisher contact and applicable mandatory consumer rights. Do not choose a governing jurisdiction, sweeping liability waiver or arbitration clause without publisher/counsel decisions.
7. Third-party distribution obligations and archive notice completeness, including framework/transitive notices; export-control declaration based on actual OS/app cryptography.
